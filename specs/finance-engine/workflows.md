# Finance Engine

## WORKFLOWS.md

---

# 1. Overview

The Finance Engine integrates with the Platform Workflow Engine to provide configurable approval workflows for financial operations.

The Finance Engine does not implement its own workflow engine.

Instead, it publishes workflow requests to the Platform Workflow Engine and responds to workflow decisions through well-defined integration contracts.

This separation ensures that approval logic remains centralized while allowing every financial process to enforce enterprise governance.

---

# 2. Workflow Objectives

The Finance Engine workflows are designed to:

- Enforce financial controls
- Prevent unauthorized financial transactions
- Support configurable approval hierarchies
- Ensure segregation of duties
- Maintain complete auditability
- Support multi-company organizations
- Support multi-branch organizations
- Support workflow delegation
- Support workflow escalation
- Support workflow notifications

# 4. Workflow Integration

The Finance Engine communicates with the Workflow Engine through service contracts.

The Workflow Engine is responsible for:

- Workflow Definitions
- Approval Levels
- Assignment Rules
- Delegation
- Escalation
- Notifications
- SLA Tracking

The Finance Engine is responsible for:

- Creating workflow requests
- Receiving workflow outcomes
- Executing approved financial actions
- Preventing execution of rejected requests

---

# 5. Workflow Lifecycle

Every Finance workflow follows the same lifecycle.

```text
Draft

↓

Submitted

↓

Pending Approval

↓

Approved

↓

Executed

↓

Completed
```

Alternative paths include:

```text
Rejected

Cancelled

Returned for Revision

Expired
```

Every state transition is recorded by the Activity & Audit Engine.

---

# 6. Workflow Statuses

The Finance Engine recognizes the following workflow statuses:

| Status           | Description                   |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Draft            | Transaction is being prepared |
| Submitted        | Awaiting workflow initiation  |
| Pending Approval | Waiting for approver action   |
| Approved         | Approved for execution        |
| Rejected         | Rejected by approver          |
| Returned         | Returned for correction       |
| Cancelled        | Cancelled before completion   |
| Executed         | Financial action completed    |
| Completed        | Workflow successfully closed  |
| Expired          | Approval period exceeded      |

Workflow status is displayed consistently throughout the Finance UI.

# 9. Manual Journal Approval Workflow

The Manual Journal Approval Workflow governs journals created directly by finance users.

Unlike journals automatically generated by business modules, manual journals may require one or more approval levels before posting.

The approval requirements are configurable through the Workflow Engine.

---

## Purpose

This workflow ensures that manually entered accounting adjustments are properly reviewed before affecting the General Ledger.

---

## Trigger

Workflow begins when a user submits a Manual Journal for approval.

---

## Workflow

```text
Create Journal

        │

Save Draft

        │

Submit

        │

Workflow Engine

        │

Finance Manager

        │

Approved

        │

Posting Engine

        │

General Ledger

        │

Completed
```

---

## Possible Outcomes

- Approved
- Rejected
- Returned for Revision
- Cancelled

---

## Business Rules

- Draft Journals may be edited.
- Submitted Journals become read-only.
- Approved Journals are automatically posted (or queued for posting based on configuration).
- Rejected Journals remain available for correction.
- Every decision is audited.

---

# 10. Journal Reversal Workflow

Journal reversals are controlled financial operations.

Posted Journals cannot be edited.

Corrections are performed through reversal journals.

---

## Trigger

---

## Workflow

```text

        │

Request Reversal

        │

Workflow Engine

        │

Finance Manager

        │

Approved

        │

Create Reversal Journal

        │

        │

Completed
```

---

## Business Rules

- Only Posted Journals may be reversed.
- Original Journals remain unchanged.
- Reversal Journals are linked to the original Journal.
- Reversal reasons are mandatory.
- Approval is configurable.

# 19. Accounting Period Closing Workflow

The Accounting Period Closing Workflow controls the process of closing an Accounting Period.

Closing an Accounting Period prevents additional financial transactions from being posted into that period.

The workflow ensures that all required financial activities have been completed before the period is closed.

---

## Purpose

The Accounting Period Closing Workflow ensures:

- Financial completeness
- Reporting accuracy
- Audit readiness
- Posting integrity

---

## Preconditions

Before a Period can be closed, the Finance Engine validates:

- No Pending Journal Approvals
- No Unposted Journals
- Bank Reconciliation Completed
- Cash Reconciliation Completed
- Ledger Reconciliation Completed
- No Outstanding Financial Exceptions

Organizations may configure additional validation rules.

---

## Workflow

```text
Finance Manager

        │

Request Period Close

        │

Finance Validation

        │

Workflow Engine

        │

Approvers

        │

Approved

        │

Close Accounting Period

        │

Activity & Audit Engine

        │

Completed
```

---

## Business Rules

- Closed Periods reject new postings.
- Period reopening requires authorization.
- Every closing operation is audited.
- Notifications are sent to affected users.

---

# 20. Fiscal Year Closing Workflow

The Fiscal Year Closing Workflow governs the transition from one Fiscal Year to the next.

Fiscal Year Closing is one of the most critical financial processes.

---

## Purpose

The workflow ensures that:

- All Accounting Periods are closed.
- Financial Statements are finalized.
- Closing Journals are posted.
- Opening Balances are prepared.
- The next Fiscal Year is activated.

---

## Preconditions

The following conditions must be met:

- All Accounting Periods Closed
- No Pending Approvals
- Trial Balance Balanced
- Financial Reports Finalized
- Closing Journals Posted

---

## Workflow

```text
Request Fiscal Year Close

        │

Validation

        │

Workflow Engine

        │

Chief Finance Officer

        │

Approved

        │

Generate Closing Journals

        │

Generate Opening Balances

        │

Activate New Fiscal Year

        │

Completed
```

---

## Business Rules

- Fiscal Years cannot overlap.
- Closed Fiscal Years are read-only.
- Reopening requires elevated authorization.
- Every action is audited.

# 24. Workflow Escalation & Delegation

The Workflow Engine manages escalation and delegation policies.

The Finance Engine respects these policies without implementing workflow logic directly.

---

## Escalation

Escalation may occur when:

- Approval SLA is exceeded.
- Assigned approver is unavailable.
- Business rules require higher-level approval.

---

## Delegation

Authorized approvers may delegate responsibilities based on organizational policy.

Delegation history is maintained by the Workflow Engine and exposed within the Finance Engine.

---

# 25. Workflow Security

The Finance Engine relies on the Authorization Engine and Workflow Engine to secure workflow operations.

Only authorized users may:

- Submit workflows
- Approve workflows
- Reject workflows
- Return workflows
- Cancel workflows
- Execute approved actions

Workflow permissions are role-based and configurable.

Every workflow action is recorded by the Activity & Audit Engine.
